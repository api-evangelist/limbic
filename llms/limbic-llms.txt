# Limbic

> Clinical AI for mental healthcare. Limbic builds regulated conversational AI that
> health plans, health systems, behavioral health providers and NHS Talking Therapies
> services embed into their care pathways to handle patient intake, assessment,
> triage and between-session support. Founded 2020; offices in New York and London.

Important: Limbic does **not** publish a public developer API, developer portal,
OpenAPI definition, SDKs, CLI or MCP server. Limbic Access is embedded into a
provider's website with a script snippet under a commercial agreement, and EHR /
patient-management integrations are delivered through customer engagements. Do not
assume or invent API endpoints, authentication schemes or client libraries for Limbic.

## Products

- [Limbic Access](https://www.limbic.ai/access): Class IIa (UKCA) medical device for
  conversational patient intake, assessment and clinical decision support. Embedded on
  a provider website; produces a full clinical report with presenting problems and
  diagnostic support.
- [Limbic Care](https://www.limbic.ai/care): clinical AI companion delivering guided CBT
  interventions and on-demand support between therapy sessions, configured per practice.
- [The Limbic Layer](https://www.limbic.ai/layer): the proprietary clinical reasoning
  layer that sits between the user and the underlying large language model to enforce
  validated clinical guidance.
